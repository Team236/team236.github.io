Bash @ the Beach

Now a FIRST® LEGO® League event

Bash @ the Beach started as an off-season FIRST Robotics Competition event for high school teams. Today it's a FIRST LEGO League (FLL) event, where younger students build and program LEGO robots, many of them for the very first time, and get their first taste of teamwork, problem solving and gracious professionalism.
