# Bash @ the Beach images

Everything you put in this folder shows up in the photo gallery on the Bash @ the Beach page (`bash.html`). Add a photo or short clip, wait a minute or two, and it's on the site. Delete it and it's gone. Until there's at least one photo here, the gallery shows "Photos coming soon" tiles.

- **Folder name:** must be exactly `bash images` (lowercase, with a space).
- **Formats:** photos `.jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif .gif` and clips `.mp4 .mov .m4v .webm`. (Convert iPhone `.heic` photos to `.jpg` first.)
- **Order:** file name order. Start names with `01-`, `02-` to choose it.
- **Caption:** the file name, minus the number and file type. `03-first-robot-run.jpg` shows as **First Robot Run**. Camera names like `IMG_4821.jpg` show no caption.
- **Hide a file:** put `_` in front of its name.
- **Size:** about 2000 pixels wide and under 1.5 MB per photo loads fast.
- **Permission:** only post photos of students whose families have agreed to it, per your school or *FIRST* media release.
